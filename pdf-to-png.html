How it works

How to convert a PDF to PNG

  1. 1

    Add your PDFs

    Choose PDF to Images and drop in one PDF or many. Locked PDFs show a padlock: click and type the password.

  2. 2

    Pick PNG and a resolution

    Choose PNG, then 96, 150, 300 or 600 DPI. Versipix shows the exact pixel size of a page before you start.

  3. 3

    Convert

    Click Convert. Each PDF gets its own folder of numbered PNGs next to it: Report-p01.png, Report-p02.png and so on.

  • Lossless pages

    PNG keeps every pixel, so small text, thin lines and sharp edges look exactly as the PDF renders them.

  • Rendered by PDFium

    The PDF engine inside Chrome draws each page, including filled-in forms and annotations.

  • Tidy output

    One folder per PDF, pages numbered with leading zeros so they sort in order everywhere.

  • Protected PDFs

    Type the password once to open the file. It is used in memory only and never saved.

Which DPI should you choose?

  • 96 DPI: screen size. Good for quick previews and thumbnails.
  • 150 DPI: sharp on screen, good for email, the web and slides. An A4 page is 1240 × 1754 pixels.
  • 300 DPI: print quality. Use it for documents you will print or for OCR.
  • 600 DPI: very fine detail, for archiving, small print and technical drawings. Files get large.

The DPI is written into every PNG, so when you print a page it comes out at its real size.

PNG or JPG?

Choose PNG for pages that are mostly text, line art, tables or diagrams: it is lossless, so edges stay perfectly sharp. Choose JPG for pages that are mostly photos, where it makes files several times smaller with no visible difference. Not sure? Convert one page both ways; your trial counts conversions, not pages.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I convert a PDF to PNG on Windows?

In Versipix choose PDF to Images, drop in the PDF, select PNG and a resolution, and click Convert. Each page is saved as its own PNG.

How do I save each page of a PDF as a separate PNG?

That is exactly what Versipix does: one PNG per page, in a folder named after the PDF, numbered in page order.

What resolution should I use for PDF to PNG?

150 DPI for screens and email, 300 DPI for printing, 600 DPI for archiving fine detail.

Can I convert many PDFs to PNG at once?

Yes. Drop in as many PDFs as you like; each gets its own folder of pages. There is no page limit.

Does it work on password-protected PDFs?

Yes. Click the locked file, type the password, and convert as normal.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Versipix converts on your PC and works offline. 10 conversions are free, then $15 once.
