How it works

How to convert a PDF to JPG

  1. 1

    Add your PDFs

    Choose PDF to Images and drop in your PDFs, or right-click them in File Explorer and open them with Versipix.

  2. 2

    Pick JPG, DPI and quality

    Choose JPG, a resolution from 96 to 600 DPI, and a quality. 90 is a sweet spot: sharp, and a fraction of the size of PNG.

  3. 3

    Convert

    Click Convert. The pages are saved as numbered JPGs in a folder next to each PDF.

  • Small, sharp files

    JPG suits pages with photos and colour. Set the quality slider to balance size and detail.

  • Many PDFs at once

    Convert a whole folder of PDFs in one go, each into its own folder of pages.

  • Prints at the right size

    The DPI is written into every JPG, so a page from an A4 PDF prints as A4.

  • Offline

    Statements, contracts and scans stay on your PC. Nothing is uploaded.

Getting the size right

At 150 DPI an A4 page becomes a 1240 × 1754 pixel JPG, usually a few hundred kilobytes: ideal for email, messaging apps and the web. Use 300 DPI if the pages will be printed. For pages that are only text and lines, PNG is often the better choice; see PDF to PNG.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I convert a PDF to JPG on Windows?

Choose PDF to Images in Versipix, drop in the PDF, select JPG and click Convert. Every page becomes its own JPG.

Can I convert a multi-page PDF to separate JPG files?

Yes. Each page is saved as its own JPG, numbered in order, in a folder named after the PDF.

What JPG quality should I use?

90 is sharp and compact for most pages. Go higher for photos you will edit, lower if size matters most.

Can I batch convert PDFs to JPG?

Yes. Add as many PDFs as you like and convert them in one go. There is no page limit.
