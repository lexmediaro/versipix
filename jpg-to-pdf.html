How it works

How to convert JPG to PDF

  1. 1

    Add the JPG

    Choose Images to PDF and drop in one JPG or several. Right-clicking images in File Explorer works too.

  2. 2

    Pick a page size

    Same as image for screens, A4 or Letter for printing and forms. Add a small or normal margin if you like.

  3. 3

    Make the PDF

    Click Make PDF. The PDF is saved next to the photo, and the original JPG is left untouched.

  • Original quality

    The JPEG is embedded as it is, not recompressed, so the PDF looks exactly like your photo.

  • Upright photos

    Phone photos are rotated using their orientation flag, so nothing comes out sideways.

  • Ready for forms

    A4 or Letter pages with a margin print cleanly and are accepted by upload forms that ask for a PDF.

  • Private

    ID cards, payslips and signed letters stay on your PC. Versipix works offline.

When a website asks for a PDF

Many forms want documents as PDF, not photos. Take a picture of the document, open it in Versipix, choose A4 with a small margin and make the PDF. Have several pages? Add all the photos at once and you get one multi-page PDF; see combine JPG files into one PDF.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I convert a JPG to PDF on Windows?

Open Versipix, choose Images to PDF, drop in the JPG and click Make PDF.

Does converting JPG to PDF lose quality?

Not with Versipix. The JPEG data is placed in the PDF unchanged, so there is no second round of compression.

Can I put several JPGs in one PDF?

Yes. Add them all and they become one PDF, one photo per page, in the order you set.

What page size should I choose?

A4 or Letter for printing and official forms, Same as image for viewing on screen.
