How it works

How to convert PNG to PDF

  1. 1

    Add your PNGs

    Choose Images to PDF and drop in PNG files or a folder. JPG, TIFF, HEIC and WebP can go in the same PDF.

  2. 2

    Choose Exact or Compact

    Exact stores each PNG lossless, so text and lines stay razor sharp. Compact saves opaque images as high-quality JPEG for a much smaller file.

  3. 3

    Make the PDF

    Pick A4, Letter or the size of each image, set the order, and click Make PDF. One PDF lands next to your PNGs.

  • Lossless when you want it

    Exact mode keeps every pixel of every PNG. Screenshots of code, charts and UI stay crisp at any zoom.

  • Transparency kept

    PNGs with transparent areas keep them in the PDF, stored lossless with their alpha channel.

  • Hundreds at once

    A folder of screenshots or scanned pages becomes one PDF in a single step. No page limit.

  • Nothing uploaded

    Screenshots often show private things. Versipix converts on your PC and works offline.

Exact or Compact: which should you use?

Use Exact for screenshots, diagrams, line art and anything with small text: PNG’s lossless compression is kept, so nothing is blurred. Use Compact for scanned photos and pages with lots of texture, where lossless storage makes a PDF several times bigger for no visible gain. Compact stores opaque images as JPEG at quality 90; images with transparency stay lossless either way.

One PDF from a whole folder of PNGs

Drop the folder and Versipix lists every image in natural filename order, so Screenshot 2 comes before Screenshot 10. Drag tiles to reorder, then make the PDF. It is named after the folder and saved beside it, or in a folder you choose.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I convert PNG to PDF on Windows without uploading?

Install Versipix, choose Images to PDF, drop in your PNG files and click Make PDF. Everything happens on your PC.

Can I combine several PNG files into one PDF?

Yes. Drop in as many PNGs as you like, or a whole folder, and you get one PDF with one image per page in the order you set.

Is PNG to PDF conversion lossless?

With Exact, yes: every pixel is kept. Compact trades a little invisible detail for a much smaller file, and only on images without transparency.

What happens to transparent backgrounds?

They are kept. Versipix stores the transparency in the PDF, so a logo on a transparent background stays that way.

Can I mix PNG and JPG in one PDF?

Yes. JPG, PNG, TIFF, HEIC, WebP, BMP and GIF can all go into the same PDF, and JPEGs are never recompressed.
