How it works

How to combine JPGs into one PDF

  1. 1

    Add your JPGs

    Open Versipix and choose Images to PDF. Drag in the photos or the whole folder, or select them in File Explorer, right-click and open them with Versipix.

  2. 2

    Set the order

    Pages follow the file names, sorted the way people count (IMG_2 before IMG_10). Drag any tile to move it, or click Sort by name to start again.

  3. 3

    Make the PDF

    Pick A4, Letter or a page the size of each photo, and a margin. Click Make PDF: one PDF, named after the folder, is saved next to your photos.

  • No quality loss

    Each JPEG is placed in the PDF as it is, byte for byte. No recompression, no softening, no colour shift.

  • Thousands of pages

    A box of scanned receipts or a whole holiday folder becomes one PDF in seconds. There is no page limit.

  • Right way up

    Phone photos carry a rotation flag. Versipix reads it, so portrait shots stay portrait in the PDF.

  • Private

    Passports, contracts and medical letters stay on your PC. Versipix works offline and never uploads.

Why your PDF stays as sharp as the photos

Most converters decode every JPEG and save it again inside the PDF. Each save throws away a little detail and can make text in scans fuzzy. Versipix copies the original JPEG data straight into the PDF instead, which is what the PDF format was designed for. The result is as sharp as your files and usually no larger than the photos added together.

Mixing JPG with PNG, HEIC or TIFF

You can combine JPG with PNG, HEIC, WebP, BMP, GIF and multi-page TIFF files in the same PDF. JPEGs always go in untouched. For the others, Compact stores opaque images as high-quality JPEG to keep the file small, and Exact keeps them lossless. Images with transparency are always kept lossless.

Choosing a page size

Same as image makes each page exactly the size of its photo, which looks best on screen. A4 or Letter fits each photo onto a standard page, centred and scaled to fit, which is what you want for printing or for sending to an office. Add a small or normal margin if the PDF will be printed.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I combine multiple JPG files into one PDF on Windows?

In Versipix choose Images to PDF, drop in the JPG files or their folder, check the order, and click Make PDF. You get one PDF with one photo per page.

Can I change the order of the pages?

Yes. Files start in natural filename order. Drag any tile to a new position before you make the PDF, or click Sort by name to reset.

Does combining JPGs reduce quality?

No. Versipix puts the original JPEG data into the PDF without recompressing it, so every page is identical to the photo it came from.

Is there a limit on how many JPGs I can combine?

No. There is no page limit and no file size cap. The free trial gives you 10 conversions, and making one PDF counts as one, however many photos it holds.

Do I need to upload my photos?

No. Versipix runs on your PC and works offline. Your photos never leave your computer.

How much does it cost?

10 conversions are free. A lifetime licence is $15 once, for 2 PCs.
