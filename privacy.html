Privacy policy

Short version: Versipix runs on your PC, collects nothing and uploads nothing.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who we are

Versipix is made and published by LEX GLOBAL IT S.R.L., a company registered in Romania (Nr. 268B, Viișoara, Bistrița-Năsăud, 420006, Romania). Contact: cj83lex@gmail.com. This policy covers the Versipix Windows app, whether downloaded from our website or installed from the Microsoft Store, and these web pages.

What the app collects: nothing

Versipix converts PDF pages to images and images to PDF entirely on your computer. It does not collect, store or transmit personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no telemetry. Your documents and images are never uploaded, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

The app opens a web page only when you click a link in it, such as the page where you can buy a licence. Those pages are covered by this policy, or by the policy of the payment provider named below.

Your documents and passwords

Versipix only reads the files you give it and writes new files next to them or in a folder you choose. It never changes or deletes your originals and never overwrites an existing file. If a PDF is protected with a password, the password you type is used in memory to open that file and is not saved anywhere.

What stays on your PC

Versipix keeps a few small files in your Windows local app data folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Versipix; for the Microsoft Store version, the app's own data folder):

  • Settings: your preferences, such as image format, resolution, page size and output folder, and the trial counter.
  • History: a list of conversions you made on this PC (file names, locations, page counts, sizes and dates), shown in the app's History page. You can clear it in the app.
  • Licence key: the key you entered, so you do not have to enter it again.
  • Log: a short text file with error messages, used only if you choose to send it to us when asking for help.

These files never leave your computer unless you send them to us yourself. You can delete them at any time by removing that folder; uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes them automatically.

Buying a licence

Licences are sold on our website and processed by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card number. We receive your name and email address to issue and send your licence key, and we keep that order record (name, email, key, order id, date, amount) for as long as the licence is valid, so we can resend the key, handle refunds and meet accounting obligations. Installing from the Microsoft Store sends us nothing.

These web pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They set no cookies and use no analytics or advertising. GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses for security and operating the service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Email

If you write to us, we keep the conversation for as long as needed to help you, and we do not add you to any list.

Children

Versipix is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you (for most people: none, or one order record), have it corrected or deleted, or object to its use. Write to cj83lex@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority; ours is the Romanian ANSPDCP.
