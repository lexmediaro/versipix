FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Versipix upload my files?

No. Versipix converts on your PC and makes no network requests of its own. There is no account and no tracking, and it works with the internet switched off.

How do I combine JPG files into one PDF?

Open Versipix, choose Images to PDF and drop in your JPGs or the whole folder. They are placed in filename order; drag the tiles to change it. Pick a page size and click Make PDF. One PDF is saved next to your photos.

How do I convert a PDF to PNG or JPG?

Choose PDF to Images, drop in one PDF or many, pick PNG or JPG and a resolution, and click Convert. Each page becomes its own image in a folder next to the PDF, numbered in order.

Is there a page or file limit?

No page limits and no file size cap. The free trial counts conversions instead: you get 10, and one conversion is one PDF turned into images, or one PDF made from images, however many pages it has.

What does it cost?

10 conversions are free, with every feature. After that a licence is $15, paid once, for 2 PCs, with free updates for version 1. No subscription. If it is not right for you, you get a full refund within 14 days.

Which image formats does it read?

JPG, PNG, multi-page TIFF, HEIC, WebP, BMP and GIF. HEIC photos need Microsoft’s free HEIF and HEVC extensions from the Microsoft Store. PDF pages are saved as JPG or PNG.

Will the images be sharp enough to print?

Choose 300 or 600 DPI for print. The DPI is written into every image, so a page prints at its real size instead of coming out blurry or huge.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Yes. Click the locked file, type its password and convert. The password is used only in memory to open that file and is never saved.

Which versions of Windows?

Windows 10 and 11, x64 and ARM. One installer covers both kinds of PC.
