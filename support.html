A file will not convert

Tell us what the app says, and if you can, attach the log: in Versipix, open Settings and click “Open log folder”, then attach log.txt. Please do not send the document itself unless you are happy to share it.

Windows warns about the installer

The setup file downloaded from this website is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer only installs Versipix for your Windows user, and you can remove it in Settings, Apps.

Common questions

  • Does it upload my files? No. Everything happens on your PC, and the app works offline.
  • Password-protected PDFs? Click the locked file in the app and type its password.
  • HEIC photos from an iPhone? Install Microsoft’s free HEIF and HEVC Video extensions from the Microsoft Store.
